AutoMate Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2025

AutoMate Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is an Android automation tool developed by 北京惠省科技有限公司 that enables users to control their devices through low-code scripting. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Important Notice: The App requests extensive Android permissions to enable user-defined automation tasks. Data is accessed only when explicitly triggered by your scripts. We do not collect or store sensitive data (e.g., passwords, banking info) unless you explicitly save it in a script.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

  • Automation scripts, text inputs, and configuration settings you create within the App.
  • User-defined data in scripts (e.g., phone numbers, messages, locations, file paths).

b. Information Collected Automatically via Permissions

The App requires broad permissions to execute automation tasks. Access occurs only during active user-defined tasks.

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, Android ID, Advertising ID.
  • Location: Precise or approximate location (via AMap SDK in China; Google Play Services globally).
  • Contacts & Communications: Contact list, call logs, SMS content (only when used in your scripts).
  • Camera & Microphone: Photos, videos, audio (only for tasks you initiate).
  • Storage: Files and media on your device (only when accessed by your automation).
  • Screen Content (Accessibility Service): UI elements, text, and screenshots for automation actions (e.g., tapping, reading text). We do not store passwords or payment details unless you save them.
  • Network & System: Wi-Fi status, battery level, app usage (for task scheduling).

c. Third-Party Services & SDKs

We integrate the following third-party services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • JPush (Aurora Push) – For push notifications
    JPush Privacy Policy
  • AMap Location SDK – For location services in Mainland China
    AMap Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Google Play version only) – Analytics & Messaging
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • XXPermissions & FreeReflection – Runtime permission handling and reflection utilities
    No personal data collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To execute your automation scripts and tasks.
  • To send push notifications (via JPush).
  • To provide accurate location services (via AMap or Google Play Services).
  • To improve App stability (anonymous crash logs via Firebase, if enabled).
  • To store your scripts and logs on your device or cloud (BandwagonHost VPS).

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

  • JPush: Shares device ID and push token for notifications.
  • AMap: Shares location data for geocoding (China only).
  • Firebase: Shares anonymized analytics (Google Play version).
  • Cloud Storage: Scripts/logs may be backed up to our BandwagonHost VPS (encrypted).
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data if required by law.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is primarily stored locally on your device.
  • Optional cloud backup uses BandwagonHost VPS with AES-256 encryption and HTTPS transmission.
  • Logs are retained for up to 30 days unless you delete them.
  • You can clear all data via App Settings > Clear Data.

5. Accessibility Service Disclosure

We use Android Accessibility Services to:

  • Simulate taps, swipes, and text input.
  • Read screen content for conditional logic in your scripts.
This data is processed only during task execution and is not uploaded unless you save it in a script. We never collect passwords, payment details, or health data without your explicit action.

6. Your Rights & Choices (GDPR/CCPA Compliant)

  • View, export, or delete your scripts and logs in the App.
  • Revoke any permission via Android Settings.
  • Disable push notifications in App or system settings.
  • Contact us at 0xFF336699@gmail.com to:
    • Access your data
    • Request deletion (right to be forgotten)
    • Opt out of data processing
    • Withdraw consent

7. Children’s Privacy

This App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If such data is found, it will be deleted immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in:

  • China (JPush, AMap, BandwagonHost VPS)
  • United States (Firebase, Google Play Services)
We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and encryption to ensure adequate protection under GDPR.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here and notified via in-app message or JPush notification. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
